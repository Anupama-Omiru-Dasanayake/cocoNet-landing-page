DESIGN MOCKUP

Integrated Crop Health Workflow

CocoGuard is being designed to support preliminary coconut disease and pest awareness through smartphone images. Its predictions must be evaluated before use in real farming decisions.

▧ PHOTO REVIEW
Image Quality

Flag unclear or poorly lit photographs and request a better image when necessary.

◈ AI ANALYSIS
Confidence Score

Present an estimated class confidence when a trained and tested model is available.

☼ FARMER SUPPORT
Clear Results

Provide understandable descriptions of visible symptoms and prediction limitations.

▤ RESEARCH GUIDANCE
Expert Guidance

Offer general management information from reliable sources, with expert confirmation for treatment.
